# Code of Conduct

This project brings together people from many countries and backgrounds to work on a sensitive topic. We want it to stay respectful, focused and safe to take part in.

## Our standards

Expected:

- Critique ideas, designs and code, not people
- Be specific and give reasons when you disagree
- Assume good faith and welcome newcomers and non-experts
- Credit other people's work
- Accept feedback gracefully

Not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks or discrimination based on nationality, religion, caste, gender, language, age, disability or any other personal characteristic
- Partisan campaigning: promoting or attacking political parties, candidates or governments, or making claims about the outcome of specific elections
- Spreading unverified accusations of election fraud
- Publishing other people's private information
- Spam, self-promotion unrelated to the project, or attempts to sell services or tokens

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, Discussions, and any place where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people from the repository when behaviour breaks these rules. Maintainers will generally give a warning first, except for serious or repeated violations.

## Reporting

To report a problem, email [adler.mason88@gmail.com]. Reports are handled privately, and the reporter's identity will not be shared without their consent.
