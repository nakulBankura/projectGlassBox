# Security Policy

This repository is early-stage research. It contains no deployed or audited voting system, and nothing here should be used in a real election.

## What to report privately

Please report privately if you find:

- A vulnerability in any code in this repository, including prototypes
- A flaw in a protocol or specification that could allow forged ballots, vote tampering, breaking ballot secrecy or coercion, and that is not already listed in the open problems or the threat model
- Sensitive personal data accidentally committed to the repository

## What can be discussed publicly

Design weaknesses, missing threats and criticism of the proposals are welcome as normal issues or Discussions. Finding flaws in the design is the purpose of this project, so you do not need to report those privately.

## How to report

1. Go to the **Security** tab of this repository.
2. Click **Report a vulnerability** and fill in the form.

Please include what you found, how to reproduce it or why it works, and its likely impact.

## What to expect

This is a volunteer-run project. We aim to acknowledge reports within 7 days and to discuss a fix or a public write-up with you afterwards. We're happy to credit you unless you prefer not to be named.

## Vulnerabilities in real election systems

If you find a vulnerability in real election equipment or systems used in India, do not post it here. Report it to the Election Commission of India or to CERT-In, the national cybersecurity agency.
